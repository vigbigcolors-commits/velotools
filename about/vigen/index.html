Vigen G.

Founder · I build VeloTools

I’m Vigen G. I write the code, ship the pages, and break the tools on my own files before anyone else does.

VeloTools exists because I got tired of uploading client photos and PDFs to random sites just to shrink a file. “We’ll delete it in 24 hours” is not a promise I can check. So I removed the upload path.

Everything useful here runs in the browser: compress, cut background, invoice, QR, PDF jobs, focus timer. Your file stays in memory on your machine. After the page loads, you can go offline and it still works.

What I care about

  • The button does the job. No account wall.
  • Numbers on the page match the tool. If it says 25 minutes, the timer is 25.
  • If two pages only differ by one word in the title, I don’t publish the second one.
  • Lab Notes are notes — not a fake magazine.

What I won’t ship

  • AI chat as the face of the site.
  • Keyword articles with no instrument behind them.
  • Medical, legal, or “guaranteed ranking” claims.
  • Watermarks on invoices or cutouts you made yourself.
