Teams attachment failures that look like Outlook problems

Many tenants still behave like a 25 MB practical attachment budget for chat and channel files — the same ballpark as Gmail, looser than Outlook’s common 20 MB consumer cap. Meeting recordings are unrelated; this page is for document PDFs people drag into chat.

Microsoft Teams PDF upload FAQ

Why won’t Microsoft Teams accept my PDF in chat?
Most often the file is over the tenant’s attachment size budget — commonly around 25 MB. Compress here, then try again. Policies can still block certain content types.
Is the Teams limit the same as Outlook’s?
Not always. Outlook.com/OWA often feels like 20 MB, while Teams chat frequently allows closer to 25 MB. Use the matching page for the app you are uploading into.
Does this tool sign into my Microsoft 365 account?
No. There is no Microsoft login on this page. You only upload the finished PDF inside Teams yourself.
Can external guests open the compressed PDF?
Yes if you attach it in a chat they can access. Link sharing still depends on OneDrive permissions — attachment sharing is usually simpler for one-off decks.
What if my admin raised the limit above 25 MB?
You can still compress for faster mobile downloads. Treat the 25 MB checker as guidance when you know your tenant allows more.
