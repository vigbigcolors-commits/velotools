Prep WeTransfer sends without a second compress upload

WeTransfer already moves the file once. Do not upload the same PDF to a random compressor beforehand. Shrink in your browser toward a practical ~25 MB email-like experience when recipients will re-download on phones — then send via WeTransfer yourself. VeloTools never hosts the payload.

Legal productions and design drops stay on your machine during compression.

Local first, WeTransfer second: one intentional cloud hop, not two.

WeTransfer’s 2 GB room vs recipients who hate huge PDFs

WeTransfer’s free tier can move on the order of 2 GB, which tempts people to send untouched 400 MB scans. Recipients then fail on mobile data and ask for “a smaller version” two days later.

A practical ~25 MB compress — Gmail-adjacent — is often the right courtesy copy even when WeTransfer would accept more. Keep the giant master for archive; send the lean PDF for reading on the go.

If you truly must send hundreds of megabytes of raw scans, WeTransfer is fine — just do not expect phone users on cellular data to celebrate the wait.

Create a WeTransfer-friendly PDF before you send

  1. Drop the oversized PDF — practical ~25 MB guidance helps even though WeTransfer allows up to ~2 GB.
  2. Compress All until the courtesy copy is easy to re-download on phones, then save it locally.
  3. Upload that lighter file to WeTransfer. Keep the full master only when the recipient explicitly needs it.

WeTransfer PDF size FAQ

Should I compress a PDF before sending it with WeTransfer?
If recipients will read on phones, yes — aim near 25 MB even though WeTransfer may allow up to about 2 GB. Keep the master for archive when needed.
Does this upload my file to WeTransfer for me?
No. Compression is local. You upload the download to WeTransfer yourself.
Can I send the full 500 MB master and a small copy?
Yes. Use two transfers or host the master on Drive/Dropbox and WeTransfer only the lean reading copy.
Will WeTransfer expire affect the compressed file?
Expiration follows WeTransfer’s rules for whatever you uploaded. Compression does not change link expiry — it only changes the local file size before you send.
Is WeTransfer more private than a free online compressor?
You still choose a cloud hop with WeTransfer. The point of this page is avoiding a second, unknown compress host before that hop.
