Zoom’s huge formal limit vs what attendees can download live

Zoom documentation can cite very large file allowances — figures near 512 MB appear in product materials — yet live meeting attendees on hotel Wi-Fi abandon anything past roughly 20 MB of PDF.

Hosts paste the giant export into chat, nobody finishes the download, and the presenter screenshares blurry slides instead. A practical ~20 MB compress before the call fixes the human problem without pretending everyone has fiber.

Keep the 512 MB master offline if you need it; share the lean handout in Zoom.

Zoom PDF sharing questions before meetings

What size PDF should I share in Zoom chat?
Aim near or under about 20 MB so attendees on weak Wi-Fi can finish downloading during the call. Zoom may allow much more — practical limits are tighter.
Is Zoom’s file limit really around 512 MB?
Product materials cite very large allowances, but that does not mean attendees will download a half-gigabyte PDF live. Use this page for the practical handout.
Can I compress without uploading the deck to Zoom yet?
Yes. Compression is local. You only upload into Zoom when you are ready to share.
Should I screenshare or send the PDF?
Screenshare the sharp original for presenting. Send a compressed ~20 MB copy in chat for people who want to keep the file.
Does this integrate with Zoom Apps?
No. There is no Zoom login or app bridge — just a local PDF shrink and a download.
